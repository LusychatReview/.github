**What is Lusychat**

Lusychat is an 18+ AI character platform for people who want a companion with a face, a name and a personality, not a blank chatbot. You browse a community library or build your own character, then talk to her in the browser and generate images when you want them.

Each character can have her own look, tone and opening line. The chat stays tied to that character, so you are not starting from zero every time you open a new tab. Pictures are generated from the same setup and spend LusyCoins. There is a free tier with a daily message limit. Paid plans raise the limit and add a monthly coin allowance.

It runs in the browser on desktop and phone. An Android app is also listed on the site. You must be 18+.
