## What it is

Lusychat is built around fictional characters rather than a single fixed companion. You pick someone from the public library or create a character with a name, look, personality and opening scene. Chat stays tied to that character. Images and short video are generated from the same setup and spend LusyCoins.

It runs in the browser. An Android app is also listed on the site.

## What you can do

- **Chat.** Talk to a chosen or custom character. The free tier includes a daily message allowance.
- **Create a character.** Name, personality, description and greeting.
- **Browse.** A large community library of characters and scenarios.
- **Images and video.** Generate stills and short clips. Higher plans add more coins and batch video.
- **Models.** Paid tiers can switch between different chat models. Coin cost depends on the model.

Prices, coin grants and model names change. Check the live plan page before you pay.

## Who it is for

Use it if you want anime-style or community roleplay, your own character, and pictures or clips in the same place. Skip it if you want a soft photorealistic dating app, therapy, or a general work chatbot.

## Pricing

There is a free tier with a limited number of daily messages. Paid plans add more messages, larger context and a monthly LusyCoins allowance for images and video. Discounts show up often. Open the upgrade screen and read the current numbers.

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- Free tier to try before paying.
- Browser, plus an Android app listed on the site.

**Cons**

- Media spends coins. Heavy image and video use costs more than the headline plan.
- Community characters vary in quality.
- Adult product. Do not put sensitive personal data into the chat.
- Not a substitute for a real person or for professional help.
